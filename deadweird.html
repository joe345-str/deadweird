📖 THE STORY

The dead have risen. They're not very good at it. Some call to apologize. Some stop to dance. Some bring pizza. Your job: survive the zombie waves, unlock increasingly absurd weapons, and figure out why there are zombie cows.

Built by Joey Donner — homeless survival blogger, founder of Catfish Heads. First game ever made using HTML5 Canvas + Claude AI. If this can be built from nothing, so can yours.

⚔️ WEAPONS GUIDE
🔫
PISTOL Always Available · Key: 1
Damage: 2 · Ammo: 24 · Fire Rate: Fast · Reload: 1.2s
Your reliable starting weapon. Fast reload, consistent damage. Best for waves 1-3 while learning patterns. Good against fast and normal zombies.
🦆
DUCK GUN Unlocks: Clear Wave 2 · Key: 2
Damage: 3 · Ammo: 18 · Fire Rate: Fast · Reload: 1.5s · QUACKS on impact
Higher damage than pistol with a fast fire rate. Fires rubber ducks that quack when they hit — the audio feedback tells you when you're landing shots. Great against fast zombies and groups.
🍕
PIZZA CANNON Unlocks: Clear Wave 4 · Key: 3
Damage: 4 + area splash · Ammo: 12 · Explosive projectile · Reload: 2s
Explodes on impact, dealing splash damage to all zombies in the blast radius. Perfect for tight groups. WARNING: don't fire at zombies right next to you — the explosion will also damage you. Excellent boss killer.
🎸
GUITAR SHOCKWAVE Unlocks: Clear Wave 6 · Key: 4
Damage: ONE-SHOTS ALL ZOMBIES · Ammo: 6 · Slow fire · Reload: 3s
Creates a shockwave that instantly kills EVERY zombie on screen. The most powerful weapon. Save it for when overwhelmed or when a boss wave hits. Only 6 shots — press R to reload between waves.
🧟 ENEMY FIELD GUIDE
Normal 🟢 HP: 2 · Speed: Medium Standard shambling zombie. Slow and predictable. Aim center-mass and fire. Good for building combos.
Fast 🔵 HP: 1 · Speed: HIGH Sprints at you. One shot to kill. Take out before it closes the distance. Duck Gun recommended — fast fire rate matches its speed.
Tank 🟠 HP: 6 · Speed: Slow Heavy armored zombie. Slow but takes many hits to kill. Use Pizza Cannon for splash damage or unload a full pistol clip.
Pizza Boy 🍕 HP: 3 · Speed: Medium Delivery zombie in a pizza uniform. Drops bonus power-up pickups when killed. Prioritize these when you need health.
BOSS 💜 HP: 20 · Every 3 waves Glows purple with aura. Massive score bonus on kill. Use Guitar Shockwave immediately when it appears. If no guitar, unload full Pizza Cannon clip then finish with Pistol.
Zombie Cow 🐄 HP: 4 · Stampede only Only appears during Cow Stampede weird event. Charges in a straight line across the map. Step out of its path — it won't turn. Shoot while it passes for easy score.

💡 PRO TIPS
🖱 FP Mouse Lock: In First Person mode, click the game canvas once to lock your mouse. Your cursor disappears and mouse movement controls aiming. Press ESC to get your cursor back.
🔄 Switch Views Freely: Press V any time during play. Top Down is easier to read the full battlefield. First Person is more intense. Switch depending on situation.
⚡ Combo Strategy: The combo timer resets quickly. Stay aggressive — constant movement and shooting builds GODLIKE combos. Guitar Shockwave on a packed wave = maximum score in one shot.
🎪 Disco Fever = Free Wave Clear: When zombies stop to dance, switch to Guitar Shockwave if you have it. If not, circle-strafe and unload highest damage weapon. All 6+ seconds are free kills.
🍕 Pizza Cannon Safety: Never fire it at a zombie within melee range — the splash will knock your own HP down. Step back, then fire. The explosion radius is about 2x your player size.
🎸 Guitar Timing: Don't waste it on 3 zombies. Save for: (a) when surrounded on all sides, (b) boss appears, (c) Disco Fever ends and a fresh wave spawns. One shot, maximum value.
🛡️ Shield Priority: When a power-up spawns, always grab the shield first if your health is below 50%. One blocked hit on a boss wave could save your entire run.
🐄 Cow Stampede: Cows run in a straight line. Move perpendicular to their path. Then shoot them from the side for easy kills. Don't try to outrun them head-on.
